BOOK SHOP — ORDERING & INVENTORY FAQ (DOC 5 — FOR RAG / KNOWLEDGE BASE)
================================================================================
How special orders, deposits, ISBN requests, and bulk/school discounts work.
Pairs with Doc 1 (roles), Doc 3 (returns on special orders), and Doc 4
(delivery).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIAL ORDERS — TIMELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• UK/US English editions in print with local distributor stock: typically 7–14
  business days from confirmed order and deposit.
• Print-on-demand or overseas-only titles: 3–8 weeks; customer told upfront.
• Academic / technical imports: highly variable; we quote a range and update
  when the supplier confirms.
• Rush where available: Extra courier fee; not all titles can be rushed.

If a supplier cancels, full deposit refund unless the customer agrees to swap
to another edition we can source.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEPOSITS & PAYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Standard deposit: 50% minimum for special orders under Rs 15,000 list; 100%
  deposit for POD, imports, or orders we flag as “non-returnable to us.”
• Balance due on pickup or before dispatch; 10-day grace after “Arrived”
  notice, then storage fee may apply (small weekly fee — GM discretion).
• Payment methods: Card, bank transfer, cash; school POs handled per Schools
  section below.

Who sets exceptions: Muhazzam Khan (GM Operations) with Priya Nair (Accounts)
for invoicing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISBN & EDITION ACCURACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Best practice: Customer provides ISBN-13 from the publisher or university
  reading list. We match imprint, year, and binding (paperback vs hardback).
• If only title + author are given: We confirm cover image and edition on email
  before ordering to avoid wrong syllabus editions.
• Multi-volume sets: Quote per volume or boxed set explicitly.

Who handles: Head Librarian (Zara Hameed) or Assistant Librarian (Omar Siddiqui)
for research; Front Desk enters the order once confirmed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN-STOCK & REORDER FREQUENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Fast movers (bestsellers, exam seasons): Weekly restock cycle where suppliers
  allow; gap-fill mid-week for top SKUs.
• Deep backlist: Ordered on customer request or librarian recommendation —
  not kept on shelf unless demand justifies it.

Who manages reorder points: Muhazzam Khan with Daniyal Raza (Inventory).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BULK, SCHOOLS & INSTITUTIONAL DISCOUNTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Schools / libraries / NGOs: Tiered percentage off list for qualifying bulk
  (minimum quantities by category — e.g. classroom sets 15+ identical copies).
  Official letterhead or purchase order may be required for invoiced accounts.
• Book clubs (registered): Modest standing discount on bulk single-title
  orders; events coordinated with James Okafor (marketing) and Zara Hameed.
• Not-for-resale inspection copies: Limited; Head Librarian + GM Front of
  House (Aitzaz Ahmed) approval; not for personal resale.

Who negotiates large deals: Aitzaz Ahmed for relationship and service terms;
Muhazzam Khan for price/margin and delivery; CEO for contracts above delegated
limits (internal finance sheet, not duplicated here).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOLDS & TRANSFERS BETWEEN BRANCHES (IF APPLICABLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• This FAQ assumes a single main branch. If a second branch opens, transfers
  add 2–4 business days and may incur a small transfer fee — update this file.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAMAGED OR SHORT-SUPPLIED DELIVERIES (INVENTORY SIDE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Receiving logs discrepancies same day; customer orders affected get priority
  message from Front Desk or Inventory.
• Credits from suppliers are tracked by Accounts (Priya Nair).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CUSTOMER-FACING FAQ (SHORT ANSWERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How long will my special order take?
A: We quote a range when you pay the deposit; most in-print titles arrive in
   7–14 business days unless import/POD.

Q: Why do you need the ISBN?
A: So you receive the exact edition your course or book club requires.

Q: Can I cancel a special order?
A: Only before we transmit to the supplier; after that, fees may apply if the
   supplier will not cancel (we explain at order time).

Q: Do you offer school discounts?
A: Yes for qualifying bulk; speak to Aitzaz Ahmed or email schools@ address
   on Doc 4 with your list.

Q: Who do I ask about a late delivery?
A: Front Desk first; they escalate to Muhazzam Khan with your tracking number.
