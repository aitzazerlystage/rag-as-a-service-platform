BOOK SHOP — INTERNAL STAFF & DIRECTORY (DOC 1 — FOR RAG / KNOWLEDGE BASE)
================================================================================
This document describes leadership and staff at the book shop. Use it to
answer questions about who works here, roles, reporting lines, and how to
reach the right person for common requests (orders, events, stock, accounts).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEADERSHIP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CEO — Dr Arshad Iqbal
Dr Iqbal sets the shop’s long-term direction, community partnerships, and
quality standards for curation and customer experience. He champions literacy
programs and author events, and is the final decision-maker on major contracts,
expansion, and policy. Day-to-day operations are delegated to the General
Managers, who report to the CEO.

General Managers — Aitzaz Ahmed & Muhazzam Khan
Aitzaz Ahmed oversees front-of-house operations: staffing rosters, customer
service standards, in-store layout, and liaison with schools and libraries for
bulk orders and reading lists. He is the usual escalation point for service
issues and floor coordination.

Muhazzam Khan oversees back-of-house operations: purchasing, supplier relations,
inventory targets, warehouse coordination, and delivery logistics. He works
closely with the inventory lead and accountant to keep margins and stock levels
healthy. Either GM can deputise for the other when one is away.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAFF (OPERATIONS, FLOOR, AND SUPPORT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Head Librarian — Ms Zara Hameed
Ms Hameed curates the fiction and non-fiction shelves, maintains the in-store
reading corner and “staff picks,” and advises customers on research projects,
curriculum tie-ins, and special orders. She coordinates with publishers for
signings and maintains the shop’s catalogue notes used at the information desk.

Assistant Librarian — Mr Omar Siddiqui
Mr Siddiqui supports cataloguing, shelf-reads, and holds management. He runs
the children’s story-hour schedule and helps schools build classroom sets.

Front Desk & Customer Service Lead — Ms Leila Farouk
Ms Farouk handles checkout, returns, gift cards, loyalty enquiries, and phone
orders. She trains seasonal staff on the POS and is the first point of contact
for general customer questions.

Inventory & Receiving Coordinator — Mr Daniyal Raza
Mr Raza checks in deliveries, updates stock counts, flags shrinkage or damages,
and coordinates with the General Manager (operations) on reorders and returns
to suppliers.

Accounts & Payroll Clerk — Ms Priya Nair
Ms Nair processes invoices, petty cash, payroll submissions, and month-end
summaries for the CEO and GMs. Vendor payment queries should be routed through
her with GM approval where required.

Marketing & Events Assistant — Mr James Okafor
Mr Okafor maintains social posts, email newsletters, window displays, and event
checklists for launches and local author evenings. He works with the Head
Librarian on programme content.

IT & Systems Support (part-time) — Ms Elena Volkov
Ms Volkov maintains workstations, backups, Wi‑Fi for events, and basic
troubleshooting for the website order feed and in-store devices.

Facilities & Safety Officer — Mr Hassan Tariq
Mr Tariq manages keys, opening/closing procedures, fire drills, first-aid kit
stock, and minor maintenance coordination with external vendors.
