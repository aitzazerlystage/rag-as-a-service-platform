PARKING & COLLECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Customer parking: 20 paid bays behind The Arcade (first hour often validated
  at till for purchases over a posted threshold — ask GM for current promo).
• Bicycles: Rack at the north side of the building.
• Click-and-collect: Yes. Order online or by phone; pickup at Front Desk with
  order code and ID. Hold time: 10 business days after “Ready” SMS/email, then
  return to stock unless customer requests extension (one extension, max 7
  days, GM-approved if busy season).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DELIVERY OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Local bicycle courier (same city, selected postcodes): Next working day for
  orders accepted before 14:00; fee tiered by distance (see checkout or till
  card). Signature or building reception may be required.
• Nationwide courier (partner TCS-style service): 2–5 business days typical;
  tracking number issued when dispatched. Rural areas may add 1–2 days.
• Heavy boxes / school pallets: Quoted separately; arranged by GM Operations
  (Muhazzam Khan) with Inventory.
• International: Not offered by default; exceptions by CEO approval only.

Delivery problems: Customer contacts us first; Front Desk logs the case. If the
courier is at fault, GM Operations coordinates compensation per courier SLA.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN-STORE SERVICES (QUICK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Gift wrapping: Small fee or free above a threshold (seasonal — ask till).
• Hold shelf: 48-hour informal holds; paid deposits extend per Doc 5.
