BOOK SHOP — STORE POLICIES & CUSTOMER SERVICE (DOC 3 — FOR RAG / KNOWLEDGE BASE)
================================================================================
Authoritative answers for returns, receipts, damaged goods, special orders,
gift cards, loyalty, and complaints. Front desk follows this; exceptions need
GM or CEO approval as stated below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RETURNS & EXCHANGES (GENERAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Time window: Full refund or exchange within 14 days of purchase for items
  in original resale condition (unmarked, unbent, no sticker residue, no
  personal inscriptions unless sold as “signed”).
• Proof: Original receipt OR loyalty-linked transaction OR card/bank reference
  we can match in the POS. Without proof, exchanges may be offered at manager
  discretion as store credit only.
• Sale / clearance: Marked “Final sale” or below-cost clearance — no refund;
  exchange for same section only within 7 days if defective (see Damaged).
• Opened shrink-wrap: Treat as used; no refund unless defective. Sealed media
  (if stocked) follows the same rule once opened.
• Gift purchases: Refund to original payment method when receipt holder
  presents receipt; otherwise store credit to the person returning the item.

Who handles: Front Desk (Leila Farouk). Disputes: General Manager — Front of
House (Aitzaz Ahmed).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECEIPTS & TRANSACTION LOOKUPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Email receipts: Offered at checkout; resend from POS within 90 days where
  the system retains the record.
• Lost receipt: We can attempt lookup by loyalty ID, date, and approximate
  time of day, or by card last four digits. Not guaranteed for cash sales
  without loyalty.

Who handles: Front Desk. Escalation for POS failures: Elena Volkov (IT) with GM
if systems are down for more than one business day.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE GOODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Customer damaged after leaving store: Not eligible unless a manufacturing
  defect appears within 14 days (e.g. missing signatures, wrong ISBN, pages
  glued shut). Head Librarian or GM may inspect.
• In-store or courier damage: Replace or refund; we log the incident. Photos
  help for courier claims.
• Print / binding defects: Exchange or refund; supplier return arranged by
  Inventory (Daniyal Raza) with GM Operations (Muhazzam Khan) when bulk.

Who handles: Front Desk first; Inventory + GM Operations for supplier returns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIAL ORDERS (POLICY SUMMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Special orders are non-returnable unless we supplied the wrong title/edition
  or the supplier cancels. Customer is told this at deposit time.
• Deposits, timelines, and ISBN rules: see Doc 5 (Ordering & Inventory FAQ).

Who advises customers: Head Librarian (Zara Hameed) or Front Desk with Zara on
complex queries. GM Operations approves unusual supplier terms.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GIFT CARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Sold in fixed denominations; redeemable for merchandise only (not for cash).
• Balance enquiries at till or by email to accounts with receipt reference.
• Lost cards: Treat like cash — not replaceable unless registered to loyalty at
  purchase (promote registration at sale).
• Expiry: Gift cards valid for 24 months from purchase date unless local law
  requires longer (extend to legal minimum).

Who handles: Front Desk; accounting queries on bulk corporate cards — Priya
Nair with GM approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOYALTY PROGRAMME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Points accrue on eligible purchases (excludes gift card purchase, some
  services, wholesale invoices). Returns reverse points from that sale.
• Tier benefits (early sale access, event RSVP priority) are published on the
  loyalty poster and newsletter.
• Data: Customers may request a loyalty statement; corrections go through Front
  Desk then Accounts if billing-related.

Who handles: Front Desk (Leila Farouk). Programme disputes — Aitzaz Ahmed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPLAINTS & ESCALATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Level 1 — Front Desk: Service wait times, wrong change, rude tone, simple
product mix-ups. Aim to resolve same visit.

Level 2 — General Manager:
• Service standards, staff conduct, loyalty disputes, layout/access —
  Aitzaz Ahmed (Front of House GM).
• Delivery delays, supplier mix-ups, stock damage patterns, courier issues —
  Muhazzam Khan (Operations GM).

Level 3 — CEO: Legal threats, media/social escalation, discrimination or safety
allegations, requests involving law enforcement, or any matter involving
potential reputational or financial exposure — Dr Arshad Iqbal.

Written complaints: Offer email to the shop (see Doc 4); acknowledge within 2
business days. Keep a brief incident log at the till for Level 2+.
